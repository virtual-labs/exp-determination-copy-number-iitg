### About the Experiment

This experiment covers the isolation of genomic DNA from mammalian cells and its separation by agarose gel electrophoresis.
The genomic DNA is digested with restriction enzymes such as EcoRI, BamHI or HindIII, denatured to the single-stranded form, and transferred onto a nitrocellulose membrane by capillary action.
The membrane is blocked with non-specific DNA and hybridised with a single-stranded radiolabelled probe complementary to the gene of interest.
The washed blot is developed by autoradiography, and the resulting band pattern is used to determine the copy number of the gene in the genome.
